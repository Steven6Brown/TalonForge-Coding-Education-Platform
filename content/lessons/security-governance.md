## Security starts at the top

**Information security governance** is the set of responsibilities and practices that the board and executive management use to provide strategic direction and make sure objectives are met. Outcomes include strategic alignment, risk management, resource management and performance measurement. Governance cycles through five processes: **evaluate, direct, monitor, communicate, assure**.

## Planning layers

- **Mission:** what the organization does. **Vision:** what it wants to become.
- **Strategic plan:** about 5 years. **Tactical plan:** 1-3 years. **Projects:** about a year. A feedback loop connects them.

Good mission statements are short, long-term and not too limiting.

## Defense in depth

Layered controls designed to **detect, delay, mitigate or prevent** unauthorized access to people, equipment, facilities and information. Physical security includes access control, perimeter protection, visibility, intrusion detection and visitor management.

## Ethics and deterrence

- **Laws** are sanctioned by a governing authority; **ethics** reflect moral standards of a group.
- **Utilitarian** reasoning weighs consequences; **deontological** reasoning weighs duty and intent.
- **Deterrence** needs fear of a penalty, a chance of being caught and a chance the penalty is applied.
